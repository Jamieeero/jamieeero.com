About Me

My parents always told me that I wanted to be an inventor from the moment I could convey it. I now attend Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute for Mechanical and Robotics Engineering. I was dually enrolled at Dartmouth College from Junior to Senior year of high school. I joined FIRST Robotics Challenge as a Freshman and spent the next 4 years learning how to design robots.

I now am pursuing a Dual Major Bachelor's degree in Mechanical and Robotics Engineering with the RPI Class of 2030. After that I hope to achieve a Master's degree in Business Administration.
