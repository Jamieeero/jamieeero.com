Eagle Scout Project

After hiking Mnt. Ascutney and taking panoramic images of the view from the top, I spent the next 2 years painting that experience. Two 15 and 18-foot long murals hung facing each other in a high-foot-traffic hallway in a Veterans Affairs hospital. Scouting America gave me a love for the outdoors and led me to experience so much in the natural world. Not everyone can have that, so I decided to make it accessible for those who gave everything to this country.
