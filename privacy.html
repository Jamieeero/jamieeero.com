Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dashboard.Jamieeero.com ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use it.

Depending on how you use the App, it may access information from third-party services that are connected to it, including:

The App only accesses information necessary for the features it provides.

Information accessed through the App may be used to:

The App does not sell personal information.

The App does not use information accessed from connected accounts for advertising or targeted marketing.

The App relies on third-party services, including Notion and Google, to provide some of its functionality.

Your use of those services remains subject to their respective privacy policies and terms. The App does not control how those third parties process information within their own systems.

The App may use API keys, access tokens, OAuth credentials, or similar authentication credentials to communicate with third-party services.

These credentials are intended to be kept confidential and used only to provide the App's functionality. They should not be shared with other people or exposed publicly.

Reasonable measures are used to prevent unauthorized access to credentials and connected account information. However, no computer system or method of transmitting or storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Information Sharing

Information accessed through the App is not intentionally sold or shared with unrelated third parties.

Information may be processed by third-party infrastructure or service providers used to operate the App, such as hosting, database, authentication, logging, or other technical service providers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the App.

You may request that information associated with your use of the App be deleted. Information may also remain in third-party services, such as Google or Notion, according to your settings and those services' own policies.

7. Security

The App uses reasonable measures intended to protect information and credentials from unauthorized access.

However, because this is a personal application and not a commercial enterprise service, it is not intended to provide any guarantee of security or availability.

You should avoid connecting accounts or providing information that you would not be comfortable having processed by the App.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically designed for children under 13. Users under 13 should not use the App without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will be made available through the App.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's handling of information, contact the person who operates the App.
